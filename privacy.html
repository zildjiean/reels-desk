Privacy Policy

Reels Desk · Last updated 30 October 2026

1. Data the app uses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives access tokens and basic profile information (such as the account or Page name) from TikTok, Google (YouTube) or Meta (Facebook). It uses the permissions needed to upload videos and read the connected account's basic information.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens and the posting history are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, database or analytics, and does not collect data from any other person.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's accounts and to show the result. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

4. Removing data

Pressing "Disconnect" in the app deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the settings of TikTok, Google or Facebook.
